Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about KeyClack features, privacy, and safety.

What is KeyClack?

KeyClack is a free, open-source mechanical keyboard sound simulator that plays realistic key and mouse switch sounds whenever you type or click anywhere on your computer.

Is KeyClack safe to use?

Yes! KeyClack is completely safe and 100% open source under the MIT license. It only reads key event codes to trigger corresponding audio slices locally. It does not record, log, or transmit any keystrokes or sensitive input data.

What platforms does KeyClack support?

KeyClack natively supports 64-bit Linux (via AppImage and binary), Windows 10/11 (x64), and macOS (Apple Silicon M-series & Intel).

Can I customize sound packs?

Yes! You can load custom sound packs, calibrate individual keys (such as using a solid thocky spacebar with crisp clicky Cherry MX Blue alphas), and adjust volumes individually.

Does KeyClack affect system performance or gaming latency?

No. Written with lightweight audio streaming, KeyClack consumes less than 1% CPU and approximately 40MB of RAM, making it perfectly safe for high-framerate competitive gaming.

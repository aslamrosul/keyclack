Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Mechvibes features and safety.

What is Mechvibes?

Mechvibes is a free, open-source mechanical keyboard sound simulator that plays realistic key and mouse switch sounds whenever you type or click anywhere on your computer.

Is Mechvibes safe to use?

Yes! Mechvibes is completely safe and 100% open source under the MIT license. It only reads key event codes to trigger corresponding audio slices locally. It does not record, log, or transmit any keystrokes or sensitive input data.

What platforms does Mechvibes support?

Mechvibes 3.3.0 natively supports 64-bit Windows (10 and 11), macOS (Apple Silicon M-series & Intel), and Linux (via AppImage and .deb packages).

Can I create custom sound packs?

Yes! You can craft your own custom packs using our web-based editor at editor.mechvibes.com and share them with the community or load them locally into the app.

How do I install sound packs?

Simply visit the Library page on our website and click "Install in Mechvibes" to install with one click, or download the sound pack folder and drop it into your custom soundpacks directory.

Does Mechvibes affect system performance or gaming latency?

No. Written natively in Rust with lightweight audio streaming, Mechvibes consumes less than 1% CPU and approximately 40MB of RAM, making it perfectly safe for high-framerate competitive gaming.
